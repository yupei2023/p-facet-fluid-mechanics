P-FACET / Fluid mechanics

When AI helps you, who is doing the thinking?

P-FACET is a six-phase routine for using AI on fluids problems without giving away the thinking. Five phases are yours. AI enters at exactly one.

The model

Six phases, one loop.

P sits at the center; F-A-C-E-T loop around it.

P

Problem-Centered Challenge You

Anchor the work in something real: a hydrant test that must not pull the main below its pressure floor.

F

Frame You

Decide what good looks like: criteria, constraints.

A

Analogize & Attempt You

Connect what you know and try it first. That draft is yours.

C

Consult & Construct You + AI

Now, and only now, bring in AI — to critique, not to solve. You set the boundary.

E

Evaluate & Explain You

Check it against evidence. Accept, revise, or reject — say why. "I checked it" doesn't count.

T

Transform & Transfer You

Make the work yours and carry the routine to the next problem set.

C is a gateway, not a moment. Find a gap later? Come back through C.

P — the problem stays at the center.

Three kinds of offloading

Handing work to a tool isn't bad. Handing over the work you were meant to learn is.

Mechanical

Side work, not what you're learning.

Fine: "Convert these five values to m³/s" — unless units are what you're practicing.

Supportive

Sharpens thinking you already did.

Better: "Here's my setup. Flag any error — the fix is mine."

Substitutive

The central thinking, done by AI.

Costly: "Find the pressure and tell me whether the test can run — show all steps."
