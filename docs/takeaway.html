Using AI on a fluids problem without giving away the thinking

The green phases are yours; AI enters only at C, the blue one.

PProblem FFrame AAttempt CConsult EEvaluate TTransfer ↺
  1. P

    Problem-Centered ChallengeYouIn one line, what's at stake? A barge that must not tip, a road that floods.

  2. F

    FrameYouKnowns, unknowns, assumptions (steady? turbulent?), and what a good answer must satisfy.

  3. A

    Analogize & AttemptYouContinuity, energy, momentum, or hydrostatics? Pick one, try it: sketch, estimate, units.

  4. C

    Consult & ConstructYou + AIShow your attempt and one gap; AI may check, hint, or explain — the rest is yours.

  5. E

    Evaluate & ExplainYouCheck it against a hand calc or the textbook — not AI. Accept, revise, or reject; say why.

  6. T

    Transform & TransferYouWrite the final answer in your own words; note what to check first next time.

At C, paste one of these. No attempt yet? Don't open ChatGPT.

Stuck at a step

Problem: [one line]. My work: [paste or photo]. I'm stuck at [step]. Flag any error (units, equation, range), then ONE hint. Don't finish it — the rest is mine.

Check my setup

My setup for [problem]: [paste or photo]. Anything inconsistent — equation, assumptions, datum? Point it out — the fix is mine.

At E, AI gets these wrong on fluids: units · assumptions · plausible nonsense · missing constraints. Check each with a calculation or a source — a second AI agreeing isn't evidence.

Four examples
  • Units: millimeters plugged into a formula written for meters.
  • Assumption: the laminar formula (f = 64/Re) used on a turbulent flow (Re = 300,000).
  • Plausible nonsense: "minor losses are negligible" — false on a short pipe.
  • Missing constraint: hit the flow target, blew past the headwater limit.

Hand AI only what the task isn't teaching. A unit conversion on the side — fine, unless units are what you're practicing; then do it yourself (mechanical). "Flag errors in my attempt — the fix is mine" — fine, you judge it (supportive). "Size the culvert, show all steps" — skips the learning (substitutive).
